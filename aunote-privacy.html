AuNote Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

AuNote keeps your records on your phone. It does not collect, sell, or share your personal information, and it does not require an account. The only data that leaves your phone is anonymous technical information sent to Google by the text reader built into the app, described below.

Who we are

AuNote is an app made by Alfred Going for keeping track of Goldback gold notes. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information the app collects

AuNote does not collect personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account.

The Android app reads photos with Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your phone. ML Kit sends Google limited technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information (such as manufacturer, model, and Android version), app information (package name and app version), a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your device, and performance and error information about the text reader. It does not include your photos, the text read from them, or your collection. Google encrypts this information in transit and does not transfer it to third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure for details.

The notes, serial numbers, amounts, and other entries you type into AuNote are saved only on your own device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

Camera and photos

When you tap Scan a note, AuNote opens your phone's camera app so you can take a picture of a Goldback; Choose a photo lets you pick a picture you already have. The picture is read on your phone to fill in details such as the serial number. The picture and the text read from it are not saved by AuNote, uploaded, or sent anywhere. You can always type the details in by hand instead.

Internet use

Apart from the ML Kit diagnostics described above, AuNote connects to the internet only to download the current price of gold from a public price service (gold-api.com). That request does not include any information about you or your collection; like any website visit, the service can see your device's internet address. The Goldback exchange rate is not downloaded: you type it in yourself, and the "Look up today's rate" link simply opens goldback.com in your browser.

Links to other websites

AuNote contains links to other websites, such as goldback.com's exchange rate and "where to spend" pages. Tapping a link opens that site in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control, so please review them if you have questions about how they handle your information.

How your information is used

Your entries are used only to show your records back to you inside the app. Nothing is used for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

Sharing with others

AuNote does not share, sell, or rent any information to third parties. The app does not contain ads. The only analytics are the ML Kit diagnostics described above, which go to Google.

If you choose to export your records (for example, to a file), that file goes wherever you send it. What happens to it after that is up to you.

Device backups

If your phone's own backup feature is turned on, Android may include AuNote's saved data in your personal device backup with Google. That backup is controlled by your phone and your Google account settings, not by AuNote.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual entries inside the app at any time. Uninstalling AuNote, or clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, removes all of its data from your device.

Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your phone's own security, such as your screen lock. Keep your phone locked to keep your records safe.

Children

AuNote is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
