How scanning works

Honest expectations, so there are no surprises.

  1. Tap Scan a note and take a picture, or choose one you already took.
  2. AuNote reads the picture on your phone, turning it sideways too, since serial numbers run up the edge of the note.
  3. The boxes it read are outlined in gold. Check them, type in anything it missed, and tap Add.

Shiny gold foil and fancy lettering are hard to read, so the scan often fills in just the serial number. A flat note, without glare, works best, and the serial is the part that matters most for spotting duplicates.

Questions

Does AuNote work offline?

Yes. Your collection, scanning and totals all work without internet. The gold price updates whenever you're online, and keeps the last price when you're not.

Where does the Goldback rate come from?

You type it in. Goldback, Inc. publishes the daily rate on goldback.com, and AuNote has a link straight to that page.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. AuNote is launching on Android first.

Where can I spend Goldbacks?

AuNote links to Goldback's own lists of places that use and exchange Goldbacks. They're a voluntary currency, so always ask a business before you pay.

Is AuNote made by Goldback, Inc.?

No. AuNote is an independent app made by a collector. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Goldback, Inc.

Help test AuNote

Before AuNote goes public on Google Play, it needs testers. All you need is an Android phone. Send the Gmail address you use for the Play Store, and you'll get an invite link to install it.
